## About Us

Arepa Lab is a Venezuelan arepa shop specializing in small-batch, handcrafted
arepas made fresh daily. Arepas are corn cakes enjoyed in Venezuela and
Colombia, served with a variety of savory fillings. Founded in 2015, we
operate three retail locations and a nationwide online shipping service.
Every arepa is prepared from scratch using locally sourced ingredients
and traditional corn dough.

- Founded: 2015
- Headquarters: Seattle, WA

## Flavors and Menu

We offer a rotating menu of classic and seasonal fillings.

- **Classics (available year-round):** Catira (shredded chicken and gouda),
  Pelua (shredded beef and gouda), Pernil (marinated shredded pork),
  Reina Pepiada (chicken, avocado, and mayonnaise), Pabellon (shredded beef,
  black beans, fried plantains, and llanero cheese), and Viuda (plain).
- **Seasonal (rotating monthly):** Roasted Pumpkin & Black Beans,
  Sweet Plantain & Cheese, and Grilled Vegetables.
- **Vegan options** are available on request. Ask staff
  about ingredients and preparation for your dietary needs.

Arepas are sold individually, by the half-dozen, and by the dozen.
Custom orders for events (weddings, birthdays, corporate) require at least
72 hours advance notice.

Use the live store menu for current availability and prices; this overview
does not guarantee that an item is in stock at the workshop counter.

## Ordering

You can order in three ways:

1. **In store**: walk in during business hours at any location.
2. **Online**: place an order on our website for pickup or delivery.
3. **Phone**: call your local store to place a pickup order.

Custom and bulk orders (25+ arepas) must be placed at least 72 hours
in advance and require a 50% deposit at the time of order.

## Delivery

These fictional delivery services are not offered by the workshop MCP counter.

We offer local delivery and nationwide shipping.

### Local Delivery

- Available within 15 miles of any retail location.
- Delivery fee: $6.99 flat rate; **free for orders over $50**.
- Same-day delivery is available for orders placed before 12:00 PM local time.
- Standard delivery windows: 10:00 AM to 2:00 PM and 3:00 PM to 7:00 PM.
- Delivery is contactless by default; leave instructions at checkout.

### Nationwide Shipping

- Arepas ship frozen in insulated packaging with dry ice.
- Shipping is available Monday to Wednesday to avoid weekend transit delays.
- Standard shipping (2-day): $14.99.
- Express shipping (overnight): $29.99.
- Free standard shipping on orders over $75.
- We currently ship only within the continental United States.

## Returns and Refunds

These fictional retail refund and cancellation windows do not apply to the
workshop counter. Only a pending real counter order can be cancelled through
MCP; staff handle questions about later states. MCP does not process payments.

Because our arepas are perishable food items, we generally cannot accept
physical returns. However, your satisfaction is guaranteed:

- If your order arrives **damaged, incorrect, or spoiled**, contact us within
  **48 hours** of delivery with a photo, and we will issue a full refund or
  send a replacement at no charge.
- **Cancellations:** Standard orders can be canceled for a full refund up to
  **24 hours** before the scheduled pickup or delivery time. Custom and bulk
  orders can be canceled for a full refund up to **72 hours** in advance;
  after that, the 50% deposit is non-refundable.
- **Refund processing:** Approved refunds are issued to the original payment
  method within 5 to 7 business days.
- Refunds are not provided for incorrect delivery addresses supplied by the
  customer or for orders left unattended after a successful delivery.
